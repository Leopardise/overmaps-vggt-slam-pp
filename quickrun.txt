QUICK RUN — OverMaps-1K evaluation with VGGT-SLAM++
===================================================

What this repo is
-----------------
We ran VGGT-SLAM++ on six OverMaps-1K scenes and three Insta360 X5 clips
to see whether the company data is usable as a SLAM benchmark. The 3.9 TB
1,000-scene dump is not here. You have:

  data/OverMaps-eval6/   the six scenes we scored (RGB + COLMAP sparse)
  data/insta360/         three 90° perspective crops + ARKit pose CSVs
  results/poses/         already-computed vanilla / optimised trajectories
  slam/                  VGGT-SLAM++ source (VGGT-1B is downloaded at setup)


Look at numbers without a GPU
-----------------------------
  cat experiments/ate_all6_results.json
  cat experiments/colmap_results.json
  cat experiments/image_quality_results_full.json
  cat experiments/trajectory_results.json
  less REPORT.md


Recompute ATE from the stored poses
-----------------------------------
Point compute_ate_all6.py at:

  SPARSE_BASE = data/OverMaps-eval6/<scene>/sparse
  OUT_BASE    = results/poses   (or keep vggt_outputs layout)

Then:  python experiments/compute_ate_all6.py


Re-run SLAM (needs CUDA, ~13 GB, VGGT-1B)
-----------------------------------------
  cd slam && ./setup.sh
  python main.py \
    --image_folder ../data/OverMaps-eval6/Waterfront_Beach_Morning_Sunny__acc84284/images \
    --log_results --skip_dense_log \
    --log_path ../results/poses/Waterfront_Beach_Morning_Sunny/poses.txt

Portrait OverMaps frames are center-cropped to 518×518 by VGGT (top/bottom
~44% FoV dropped). That warning is expected.

Insta360: frames in data/insta360/*/images are already forward 90° crops.
GT is poses_*.csv (phone ARKit), not COLMAP.
